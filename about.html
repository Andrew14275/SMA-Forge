How We Made It

Our Methodology

A structured, four-step process from topic selection to deployment — designed to balance research depth with presentation quality.

01

Topic Research & Scoping

We began by identifying Shape Memory Alloys as our focus material — chosen for their relevance to SDGs 3, 9, and 12, and their strong cross-disciplinary appeal across chemistry, physics, and engineering. Academic papers, NCERT references, and peer-reviewed journals formed our primary sources.

02

Information Architecture

Content was organised into six logical sections — Concept, Phases, Applications, History, SDGs, and About — each designed to stand alone while building on the previous. We mapped the learning journey before writing a single line of content or code.

03

Design & Development

Using a custom design system (SMA Forge tokens), we built each page as a real, separate HTML file — not a simulated single-page app. Dark mode, responsive layouts, scroll animations, and a light/dark toggle were implemented for accessibility and polish.

04

Review, Testing & Deployment

All pages were reviewed for factual accuracy, tested on mobile and desktop browsers, and cross-checked against the project rubric. The site was deployed to GitHub Pages for public access, with a companion Chemistry Quiz site linked from the homepage.

Purpose & Value

Why SMA Forge Exists

Most resources on Shape Memory Alloys are either too technical for students or too shallow to be useful. We wanted something in between.

📚

Curriculum Aligned

All content is grounded in class-level chemistry — covering phase transitions, alloy chemistry, and material science concepts that appear in standard syllabi.

🌍

SDG Connected

We link material science directly to real-world sustainability goals — showing how a single alloy can impact healthcare, infrastructure, and responsible manufacturing.

🎯

Visually Interactive

Phase diagrams, timelines, and interactive toggles replace dense paragraphs. We believe visual learning accelerates understanding of abstract concepts like crystal transformation.

🔓

Freely Accessible

Deployed on GitHub Pages with no login, no paywall, no ads. Any student with a browser can access the full site — that's the point of open educational resources.

Acknowledgements — We thank our chemistry faculty for guiding the scope of this project and for encouraging us to present scientific content in a format that goes beyond a traditional report.
