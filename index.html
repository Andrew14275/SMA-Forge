Quick Introduction

What Is a Shape Memory Alloy?

Shape Memory Alloys (SMAs) are a unique class of smart metallic materials that can recover their original shape after seemingly permanent deformation — simply by being heated above a critical temperature.

The most widely used SMA is Nitinol (Nickel-Titanium), discovered at the Naval Ordnance Laboratory in 1962. It can recover strains of up to 8–10% — compared to just 0.5% for ordinary steel.

Effect 1
Shape Memory Effect
Deform at low temp → heat → returns to original shape.
Effect 2
Superelasticity
At body temperature — deform and spring back without any heating needed.
